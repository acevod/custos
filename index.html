Custos

Structural risk monitor for Bitget stock rTokens — watches wrapper liquidity, not price direction.

The problem

A tokenized stock's price can look fine while the wrapper underneath it quietly becomes harder to trade — spread widens, depth thins, volume dries up. Custos watches that structure, not the price, and decides whether to hold, act, or wait based on it alone.
