Custos

Structural risk monitor for Bitget stock rTokens — watches wrapper liquidity, not price direction.

The problem

A tokenized stock's price can look fine while the wrapper underneath it quietly becomes harder to trade — spread widens, depth thins. Custos watches that structure rather than betting on price direction, and decides whether to hold, act, or wait based on it alone.

Score: 0 (worst) to 1 (best) wrapper health. A “warming up” bar shows progress toward a 48-hour baseline; “no fresh signal” means the feed is unchanged since the last cycle.

Decision log

Every cycle, the agent evaluates the weakest holding and the strongest cash position and logs the outcome — whether or not it results in a trade. Most evaluations are settled by fixed safety rules; the model's own reasoning appears only when it was actually consulted.

Performance

Two views, kept separate on purpose. Round trip = a sell followed by its buy-back, measured against simply holding through it (“gain vs holding”). Exit = the sell price against the original entry, which ignores what re-entering cost. Neither predicts price direction; the point is whether stepping aside from structural stress helped.

Run records

Every executed trade — timestamp, instrument, direction, price, quantity, and the resulting change to the USDT balance. Paper trading, no real funds involved.
